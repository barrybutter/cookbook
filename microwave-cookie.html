Here is my easy way to enjoy a late evening, whilst eating good!

This is how you can make my iconic microwave cookie!

Ingredients:

1.5 tablespoons butter

1 tablespoon sugar

1 tablespoon brown sugar

DISCLAIMER: If you dont have brown sugar, or white sugar, you can just use 2 tablespoons of whatever sugar you have!

0.125 teaspoons baking powder

0.5 teaspoon vanilla sugar (optional)

1 teaspoon milk

Enough flour (Idk, like 5-10 tablespoons, i dont measure)

Baking:

1. Melt your butter

2. Put your sugar in (Both types, if you use both)

3. Baking powder and if oyu have vanilla sugar, put that in

4. Insert milk

5. Add flour, start small with flour, and keep mixing more and more flour in until it turns into a good consitency

Now if you dough gets too dry, just add some more milk. Too wet now? Just add flour

6. Take your dough and put in on a plate with parchment paper or some sort of sandwich paper

7. Microwave for about 1-2 minutes

Enjoy!
